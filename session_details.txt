Taming Rogue Agents: Observability-Driven Evaluation for Production Reliability

Session Description: 
Moving from a viral demo to a reliable enterprise AI application is the hardest hurdle in modern software engineering. Because agents are inherently non-deterministic, validating their performance requires a granular look at the why and how behind every action. 

This session explores how observability redefines agentic evaluation beyond binary pass/fail testing, shifting the focus from the result to the entire reasoning chain. We dive into a playbook to ensure your agents aren’t just getting lucky, but are following the correct execution path for real-world tasks.

What you’ll learn:
-	Beyond Output: Why judging agents on final answers alone is a recipe for silent failure in production. 
-	Handling Non-Determinism: Using observability to build confidence in unpredictable agentic workflows.
-	The Playbook: A practical, incremental framework for implementing evaluation-first development.
Are you ready to stop guessing and start measuring? Join us to master the art of agent evaluation.

Details:

In this session, we bridge the gap between AI research and product engineering. We will explore the "Evals-First" methodology used by industry leaders to build agentic systems that work reliably at scale. Attendees will learn how to move beyond basic prompting and build robust, automated evaluation harnesses that catch compounding errors before they hit the user. 

We provide a deep dive into the shift from traditional AI monitoring to agent-centric observability where static test sets are no longer sufficient - introducing a practical playbook for validating non-deterministic reasoning chains, ensuring agents aren’t just getting lucky, but are following safe, efficient, and logical paths to their results.

Attendees will learn to visualize agent "inner monologues," detect hallucination loops, and implement automated, model-graded evaluations to build production-ready AI applications.
